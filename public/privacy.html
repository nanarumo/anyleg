Anyleg Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Anyleg is a free-to-play sports and casino parlay simulator for adults 18+. It uses play money only. There is no real-money gambling, and nothing in the app can be bought, deposited, withdrawn or exchanged for real money, prizes or anything of value.

Data we collect

To run the game fairly, Anyleg keeps your play-money wallet on our server. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account for your device. We do not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts, location or any payment details. The server stores:

  • an anonymous account ID for your device
  • the username you choose
  • your play-money balance, bets, casino rounds, rewards and level progress
  • your referral code and, if you used one, the code of the player who referred you
  • your email address, only if you choose to add one, and when you verified it (for the Founders reward)

We use your email only to sign you in with a one-time code and to record your Founders reward; we never send marketing. We use the rest only to run the app: keeping your balance, settling bets and paying rewards. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. It is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it only on our behalf and protects it to the same standard. Like any web service, our server and ESPN see your device's IP address when the app connects; we don't use it to identify you.

Data stored on your device

Your avatar, settings and a copy of your recent history are also saved on your device so the app opens quickly.

Network requests

The app downloads public sports scores and odds from ESPN's public APIs. These requests contain no account or personal information. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to ESPN, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Anyleg is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any sports league, team, player, sportsbook or casino. Names belong to their owners. The app does not show league or team logos or player photos.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile, then Login & Security, then tap Delete account. This permanently erases the account and everything tied to it from our server and your device, right away. We keep account data only until you delete the account. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Anyleg is not directed to children and is only for adults 18 and older. A birth-year check runs on first launch.
